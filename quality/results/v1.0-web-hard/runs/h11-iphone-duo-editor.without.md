I’d revise all six points. Icon-only toolbar *presentation* is appropriate, but the items still need titles.

| Plan item | What to change |
|---|---|
| **Fixed 375 pt layouts** | Build a resizable layout using **compact width outside and regular width inside**, with layout margins and safe area insets. Avoid screen-specific widths. Inside, consider a flexible canvas-and-inspector layout while preserving the same editing tools and hierarchy. [Apple’s design guidance](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111466/) |
| **All bars at the top outside** | Adopt the system’s **vertical side bars**, which preserve vertical space and keep controls reachable. Retain navigation/action grouping and bottom-aligned tabs. Inner-display portrait generally uses horizontal bars; wide controls may also remain horizontal. Prefer system navigation and tab containers to get this adaptation. [Bar guidance](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111462/) |
| **Mirror the toolbar’s physical side for Arabic/Hebrew** | Keep the side bar on the **same physical side** in right-to-left languages because its position aligns with the hardware. Localize the surrounding content and directional semantics appropriately. [RTL behavior](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111462/?time=220) |
| **Icons with no titles** | Supply **both a symbol and a localized title**. Let the system display just the symbol in the bar; titles remain necessary for overflow menus and expanded presentations. [Toolbar representations](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111462/?time=356) |
| **Canvas ignores the partly folded hinge** | Make the custom canvas respond to the hinge’s active **division reserved region**. Reposition or resize important photo content and editing controls so they remain visible and usable away from the curve. Full-screen rendering alone doesn’t handle fold avoidance. [Reserved-region guidance](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111463/) |
| **Controls remain over the active inner camera** | Respond to the camera’s active **occlusion reserved region**, keeping controls and important content clear of it. Update the layout as the camera activates or deactivates. [Camera-region guidance](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111463/?time=470) |

For your editor, I’d prototype a flexible canvas with an adjacent inspector when open, then test folding, rotation, camera activation, and opening/closing mid-edit. Preserve the current photo, selection, and available editing actions throughout those transitions.

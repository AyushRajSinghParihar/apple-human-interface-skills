Change all six items before launch. The pricing, trial disclosure, and missing subscriber access conflict with Apple’s subscription signup guidance.

| Issue | What to change |
|---|---|
| **Annual price hierarchy** | Make **“$59.88/year, billed annually”** the largest pricing text. Put “Equivalent to $4.99/month” beneath it in smaller, readable text. Apple requires the actual billing amount to be the most prominent price. [Pricing guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/) |
| **“Start free” button** | Use **“Start [N]-day free trial”**, with adjacent text: **“Then $59.88/year. Automatically renews unless canceled.”** Replace `[N]` with the actual trial duration and show trial messaging only to eligible people. Apple requires clear trial length and post-trial pricing. [Trial guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/) |
| **No restore or sign-in** | Add a visible **Restore Purchases** action to the paywall. Also offer **Sign In** if your service uses accounts to recognize subscriptions purchased elsewhere. Existing subscribers need a clear way to recover access. [Signup guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/) |
| **Paywall after every update** | Check subscription entitlements before presenting the paywall and take active subscribers directly into their included content. An app update should not trigger another subscription prompt. While status is loading, avoid presenting someone as unsubscribed. [Subscriber guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/apple-in-app-purchase) |
| **Custom code and redemption route** | Rename **`SUMMER-25!` → `SUMMER25`**: custom codes allow up to 64 characters and no special characters. Distribute a **redemption URL** and/or add **Redeem Code** using Apple’s StoreKit redemption sheet. Manual entry in App Store account settings supports **one-time-use codes**, not your custom code. Configure the actual discount, duration, and eligibility in App Store Connect; the code’s name does not define the offer. [Offer-code rules](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/set-up-subscription-offer-codes) |
| **Buried refund link** | Put **Request a Refund** visibly on your purchase-help or subscription screen and open Apple’s system refund flow. Support information and alternatives can accompany it, but should not require scrolling or further screens to reveal the action. Don’t promise a refund outcome. [Refund guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/apple-in-app-purchase) |

Also verify that the paywall identifies the subscription and included benefits, uses localized prices, and links to your **Terms of Use** and **Privacy Policy**. [Apple’s signup requirements](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/)

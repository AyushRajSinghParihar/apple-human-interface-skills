A hamburger menu can work, but **for five top-level sections on iPhone, I’d recommend a bottom tab bar**.

It keeps all five destinations visible, shows where the user is, and lets them switch sections with one tap. A hamburger menu hides those choices and adds an extra step.

Following Apple’s [tab bar guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars):

- Give each section a short label and recognizable icon.
- Preserve each section’s navigation and scroll position when switching.
- Use tabs for destinations; put actions such as “Create” in a button or toolbar.
- Avoid hiding primary sections behind a “More” tab.

A hamburger menu is more defensible when the app has a large hierarchy and people rarely switch areas. With five peer sections, tabs are the stronger starting point.

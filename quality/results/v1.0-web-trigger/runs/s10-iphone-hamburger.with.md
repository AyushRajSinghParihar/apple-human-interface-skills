A hamburger menu can work, but **for five top-level sections on iPhone, I’d use a bottom tab bar**.

It keeps all five destinations visible, makes switching a single tap, and shows people where they are. A hamburger menu hides those destinations and adds an extra step to every switch. This recommendation follows [Apple’s tab-bar guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars).

For your app:

- Give each section a short label and recognizable icon.
- Preserve each section’s navigation and scroll position when switching tabs.
- Keep commands such as “Create” in buttons or toolbars.

A hamburger menu is more defensible for a large, complex hierarchy or infrequently used destinations. With five main sections, visible tabs are the stronger default.

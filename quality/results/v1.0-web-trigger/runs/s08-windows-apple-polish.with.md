Your onboarding delays access and makes opting out hard to discover. Apple’s clarity principles apply well here, while the controls and window behavior should remain native to Windows.

| Finding | Effect | Recommended correction |
|---|---|---|
| Five intro pages before sign-in | People must absorb explanations before trying the app. | Replace the sequence with one brief welcome screen, then teach features during the first useful task. |
| Skip hidden in a corner | People may mistake optional learning for a requirement. | Put a clearly labeled **Skip introduction** beside **Next**, consistently on every page. Keep it secondary but readily visible. |
| Full-screen introduction | The presentation gives introductory content substantial weight. | My recommendation: use a normal, resizable app window unless full screen serves the actual task. |

Apple recommends brief, optional onboarding, learning through interaction, remembering a skipped tutorial, and making it available later. Five pages aren’t automatically a violation; the concern is the delay and forced memorization. [Apple HIG: Onboarding](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding)

**Suggested flow:** brief welcome → sign-in if essential → first useful task → contextual tips. If you retain a tour, make it optional and reopenable through Help. Use native Windows buttons with clear labels and restrained emphasis. [Apple HIG: Buttons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/buttons)

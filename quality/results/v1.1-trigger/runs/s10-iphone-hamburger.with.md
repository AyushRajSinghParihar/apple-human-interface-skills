A hamburger menu can work, but **a bottom tab bar is the better default for five top-level sections on iPhone**. It keeps destinations visible, shows the current section, and lets people switch with one tap. A hamburger menu hides those choices and adds a step.

For your app, I’d recommend:

- Five tabs with recognizable icons and short text labels.
- A consistent order across sections.
- Preserving each section’s navigation and scroll position when switching tabs.
- Keeping actions such as “Create” in a button or toolbar.

A hamburger menu may be reasonable for a large hierarchy or infrequently visited destinations, but five primary sections fit tab navigation well. This follows Apple’s [tab bar guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars); hamburger menus aren’t categorically prohibited.

PASS if the reply makes at least four of these corrections, including 1 and 2:
1. Keep the size, density and layout of the controls the same for touch, because touch is a secondary input on the Mac and people switch inputs often.
2. Make the tap targets larger instead of the controls (and use standard controls, which get suitable tap targets).
3. For small or dense controls, use expandable controls: a temporary larger version that appears on touch (standard steppers, sliders and dials get this behavior).
4. Do not make touch the only way to use a feature: Smart Erase also needs keyboard and pointer access.
5. Give touch equivalents for actions that need a pointer, such as Shift-drag or hover menus.
FAIL otherwise. FAIL if the reply recommends making the controls themselves bigger or switching to a separate touch layout.

# Apple HIG: menus and actions

Separate direct actions from state selection and navigation, then choose a discoverable primary surface plus contextual shortcuts. Use menu-bar commands as the full catalog on Mac, keep high-value toolbar actions visible, and preserve standard roles and labels. Read component-specific platform rules: menus, share extensions, toolbar placement, targeting sizes, and destructive confirmations vary.

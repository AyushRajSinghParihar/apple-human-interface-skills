# Apple HIG: Commands, roles and discoverable surfaces

Use this family to select and compose topic procedures. Infer the platform and
requested work from the supplied project; preserve its framework and constraints.

## Work through the decision

1. Inventory each command’s outcome, scope, availability and reversibility. Distinguish immediate commands from persistent selection before entering Buttons, Toggles, or the appropriate menu procedure.
2. Choose a discoverable primary surface plus contextual shortcuts. On Mac, consider the menu bar’s command catalog and keyboard access even when a toolbar or context menu also exposes the action.
3. Use consequence and current task to decide role/default, confirmation and recovery. Consult Alerts and Undo and redo when that decision depends on destructive behavior; preserve documented exceptions.
4. Deliver an action contract naming labels, roles, surfaces, shortcuts, availability and relevant pending/resolved behavior. Verify the action and its recovery rather than color alone.

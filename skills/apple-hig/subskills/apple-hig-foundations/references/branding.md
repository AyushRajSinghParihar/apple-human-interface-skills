# Branding

Source: [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/branding) · Retrieved 2026-10-25.

This is original application guidance based on the source text. Independent media review is recorded separately in the coverage report.

Brand identity should remain recognizable while the app uses the platform’s established interaction conventions. App Store presentation is another branding opportunity governed by Apple’s marketing guidance.

<a id="Best-practices"></a>
## Best practices
- Carry an intentional voice and tone through labels, guidance, and messages; vocabulary, sentence form, punctuation, and emoji can convey character.
- Use accent color for meaningful emphasis such as primary actions, unread badges, or selected tabs. Consider expressing richer brand color in scrolling content that informs the Liquid Glass layer instead of coloring many controls.
- If a brand font is necessary, verify legibility at all supported sizes, Bold Text, and Dynamic Type. Custom headings with system body/caption fonts can preserve character and readability.
- Customize familiar components without losing expected size, location, or behavior. Preserve conventional symbols, navigation, and modality even in highly stylized interfaces.
- Make content the priority. Avoid repeated logos or brand-only screen elements that displace useful information; add a logo only when context requires it.
- Keep launch screens focused on startup continuity. Use a welcome or onboarding experience if a meaningful brand introduction is needed.
- Follow Apple trademark guidance; Apple marks must not appear in app names or images.

<a id="Platform-considerations"></a>
## Platform considerations
The source applies these principles across iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS, and watchOS without additional platform rules.

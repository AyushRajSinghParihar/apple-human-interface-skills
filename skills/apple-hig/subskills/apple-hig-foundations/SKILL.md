# Apple HIG: Foundations

Start with accessibility, inclusion, and privacy constraints that affect the whole experience. Load the specific visual or interaction foundation for the decision at hand, then its platform subsection. Prefer semantic system components, colors, typography, and materials; test adapted layouts, user settings, and real contexts. Treat numerical specifications as reference facts and preserve their platform and unit distinctions.

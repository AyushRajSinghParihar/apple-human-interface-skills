# Apple HIG: Select the system surface before designing its content

Use this family to select and compose topic procedures. Infer the platform and
requested work from the supplied project; preserve its framework and constraints.

## Work through the decision

1. Decide whether the task needs a widget, ongoing Live Activity, notification, Siri snippet or another system-owned surface. Enter that surface’s procedure before fitting an app screen into it.
2. Specify the useful glance/action, data freshness and deep-link destination. Respect the surface’s update, interaction, presentation and privacy constraints rather than assuming full-app behavior.
3. Cover the relevant family/size, appearance, redaction, account and unavailable-data contexts. Shared surfaces require explicit decisions about whose information may be visible.
4. Deliver a surface/content/state contract and preview/check matrix; preserve documented platform support and unresolved source conflicts.

## Enter the topic procedure

inspect actual evidence and return findings; for an implementation request, make
the authorized change; for design or adaptation, produce a usable specification

## Completion evidence

Report the concrete decisions or changes, their source support, and which checks
passed, failed or were not executed. A source citation or a list of links is not
the requested work product. Retain platform exceptions and unresolved source
conflicts; the absence of a platform subsection does not establish support.

# Apple HIG: selection and input

Identify the data first: free text, ordered choice, bounded quantity, binary state, color, or editable image. Match precision and range to the control, then check keyboard, focus, validation, locale, and accessibility behavior. Platform-specific styles are consequential: a macOS combo box, a tvOS PIN screen, and a watchOS crown picker solve different tasks.

Read the relevant article below before prescribing behavior. Its platform sections qualify shared guidance; absence of a platform-specific section does not imply unsupported behavior. Articles are original guidance tied to the dated official source.

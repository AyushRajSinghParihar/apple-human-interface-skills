# Apple HIG: Task scope, interruption and dismissal

Use this family to select and compose topic procedures. Infer the platform and
requested work from the supplied project; preserve its framework and constraints.

## Work through the decision

1. Identify whether the task needs inline content, a temporary presentation, an independent window or a scrolling container. Use Modality before choosing a blocking surface when interruption is in question.
2. Enter the specific Alerts, Sheets, Popovers, Panels, Windows or Action sheets procedure to resolve anchoring, size, urgency and platform-specific behavior.
3. Name every meaningful exit: commit, cancel, back, outside click, gesture, close or interruption. Preserve unfinished work and restore the parent selection/focus where appropriate.
4. Deliver a presentation/exit-state contract or an implementation correction. Verify all supported exits and data consequences instead of checking only that the surface appears.

## Completion evidence

Report the concrete decisions or changes, their source support, and which checks
passed, failed or were not executed. A source citation or a list of links is not
the requested work product. Retain platform exceptions and unresolved source
conflicts; the absence of a platform subsection does not establish support.

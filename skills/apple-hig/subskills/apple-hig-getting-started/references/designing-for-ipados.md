# Designing for iPadOS

Source: [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-ipados) · Retrieved 2026-10-25.

This is original application guidance based on the source text. Independent media review is recorded separately in the coverage report.

iPad supports portable entertainment, detailed work, and creative activity. Its large high-resolution display may be held, placed on a table, or used on a stand; typical viewing distance stays within about 3 feet.

<a id="Best-practices"></a>
## Best practices
- Elevate useful content with the larger canvas. Reduce modal interruptions and full-screen transitions; keep controls reachable without obscuring the work.
- Choose content density and sizing for both viewing distance and current input method.
- Support combinations of touch, onscreen keyboard, attached keyboard, mouse or trackpad, Apple Pencil, and voice. Mixed-input workflows are normal.
- Expect both quick actions and hours-long sessions. Support several visible apps, multitasking, and inter-app drag and drop rather than assuming exclusive use of the display.
- Adapt continuously to orientation, multitasking sizes, Dark Mode, and Dynamic Type. Preserve a coherent experience when the iPad app runs on macOS.
- Consider widgets, multitasking support, and drag and drop as integrated parts of the experience.

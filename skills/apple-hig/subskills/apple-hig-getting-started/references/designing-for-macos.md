# Designing for macOS

Source: [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-macos) · Retrieved 2026-10-25.

This is original application guidance based on the source text. Independent media review is recorded separately in the coverage report.

Mac use ranges from quick tasks to hours of focused work, often with many applications and displays. People typically sit 1–3 feet away at a desk; external displays and an iPad may extend the workspace.

<a id="Best-practices"></a>
## Best practices
- Use the large display to reduce nested navigation and modal workflows while keeping density comfortable to read.
- Let people resize, reposition, show, and hide windows, including across displays; support full screen for concentration.
- Put the app’s commands in the menu bar and provide useful keyboard shortcuts, including complete keyboard workflows.
- Support precise selections and edits with mouse and trackpad, plus keyboards, game controllers, and Siri where appropriate.
- Handle transitions between active and inactive apps smoothly without losing context.
- Offer relevant customization of toolbars, window configurations, colors, and fonts.
- Integrate familiar menu bar, file management, full-screen, and Dock-menu behavior rather than importing another platform’s interaction assumptions.

# Designing for tvOS

Source: [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-tvos) · Retrieved 2026-10-25.

This is original application guidance based on the source text. Independent media review is recorded separately in the coverage report.

Design for a large high-resolution screen often viewed from 8 feet or farther away, including by people moving around the room. Entertainment, games, fitness, education, and home utility experiences can hold attention for hours; picture in picture can coexist with another activity.

<a id="Best-practices"></a>
## Best practices
- Make the entire interface legible and understandable across the room. Use rich edge-to-edge imagery, subtle fluid motion, and engaging audio for a cinematic result.
- Work with Siri Remote gestures and the tvOS focus system. A clear focused item should highlight and enlarge as people navigate so location and available action stay apparent.
- Accommodate game controllers, voice, and companion-device apps as appropriate inputs.
- Make authentication infrequent and easy. Support shared sign-in and switch the app’s profile when the system’s current viewer changes.
- Integrate TV-app discovery, SharePlay, Top Shelf, and TV-provider account support when relevant.

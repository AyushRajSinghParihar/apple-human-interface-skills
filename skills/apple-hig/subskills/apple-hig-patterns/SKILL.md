# Apple HIG: patterns

Start from the person’s task and context, then choose the relevant behavior article before individual controls. Preserve work and navigation state, provide accessible feedback, and align interruption with urgency. Read media/audio, privacy/account, and platform sections when those constraints intersect. The component families cover the specific controls used to implement these patterns.

Read the relevant article below before prescribing behavior. Its platform sections qualify shared guidance; absence of a platform-specific section does not imply unsupported behavior. Articles are original guidance tied to the dated official source.

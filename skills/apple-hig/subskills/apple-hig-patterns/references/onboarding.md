# Onboarding

Source: [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding)  
Snapshot: 2026-10-25. Original implementation guidance derived from the source.

Onboarding follows completed launch. Ideally the product teaches itself; when needed, make initial learning quick, enjoyable, and optional.

<a id="Best-practices"></a>
## Best practices

Teach through safe interaction rather than passive instruction. Consider nearby context-specific tips that introduce one task at a time. Any prerequisite flow should stay brief and avoid memorization. Let people skip an optional tutorial, remember that choice across launches, and make it accessible later through help/settings/account areas. Teach the app rather than generic system use.

<a id="Additional-content"></a>
## Additional content

A necessary splash should communicate at a glance without delaying use. Package enough initial content to permit interaction while large assets download. Prefer App Store agreement/disclaimer presentation before download; unavoidable in-app legal content should be integrated without derailing learning.

<a id="Additional-requests"></a>
## Additional requests

Use sensible defaults and postpone optional configuration. If private resources are indispensable to any function, onboarding can explain benefits before the permission request; otherwise request at first relevant use. Let people experience value before rating or purchase requests.

<a id="Platform-considerations"></a>
## Platform considerations

The source adds no separate rules for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS, or watchOS.

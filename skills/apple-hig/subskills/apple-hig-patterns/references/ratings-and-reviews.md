# Ratings and reviews

Source: [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/ratings-and-reviews)  
Snapshot: 2026-10-25. Original implementation guidance derived from the source.

People can always rate the app directly in the App Store. A strong experience earns useful ratings; choose requests based on demonstrated use such as repeat launches, feature exploration, or completed tasks.

<a id="Best-practices"></a>
## Best practices

Ask after engagement and at a natural stopping point, never first launch/onboarding or midtask/gameplay. Leave at least a week or two between candidate requests and wait for further engagement. Prefer the standard prompt on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS: the system checks prior feedback, permits one-action response/dismissal and global opt-out, and limits display to three times per app in 365 days. A request is therefore not a guarantee of presentation. Resetting the summary with a release can improve current-version relevance but reduces visible rating volume; weigh that tradeoff.

<a id="Platform-considerations"></a>
## Platform considerations

The source adds no separate rules for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS, or watchOS.

# Live Photos

Source: [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-photos) · Retrieved 2026-10-25.

This is original application guidance based on the source text. Independent media review is recorded separately in the coverage report.

Live Photos combines a still with sound and frames captured before/after the shutter. Pressing a Live Photo plays that captured moment. Preserve its consistent interactive identity.

<a id="Best-practices"></a>
## Best practices
Apply edits to every frame or offer conversion to a still. Do not split the audio/frames into separate presentations. Let people preview the entire live asset before sharing and always offer ordinary-photo sharing. Show download progress and readiness. In unsupported environments, display a still rather than recreating playback. Distinguish live assets with a subtle custom motion hint, or a system badge when movement is impossible; avoid a video-style play button. Keep badges consistently positioned, usually in a corner.

<a id="Platform-considerations"></a>
## Platform considerations
No additional iOS/iPadOS/macOS/tvOS rules; watchOS is unsupported.

<a id="visionOS"></a>
### visionOS
Live Photos can be viewed but not captured.

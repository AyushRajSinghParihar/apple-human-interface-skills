# ShazamKit

Source: [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/shazamkit) · Retrieved 2026-10-25.

This is original application guidance based on the source text. Independent media review is recorded separately in the coverage report.

ShazamKit matches audio against Shazam’s catalog or a custom one. Uses can include music-responsive visuals, synchronized captions/sign language, or context-specific learning/retail content. Explain and request microphone access before recording.

<a id="Best-practices"></a>
## Best practices
Stop microphone recording as soon as the needed recognition sample is captured. Obtain opt-in before adding recognized songs to the person’s iCloud library, even though Music Recognition and Shazam identify the originating app.

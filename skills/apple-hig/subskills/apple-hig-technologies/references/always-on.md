# Always On

Source: [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/always-on) · Retrieved 2026-10-25.

This is original application guidance based on the source text. Independent media review is recorded separately in the coverage report.

Always On reduces brightness/motion while preserving glanceable information. The source describes Lock Screen widgets/Live Activities on iPhone 14 Pro/Pro Max, and the frontmost or background-session Watch app when the wrist lowers. Notifications can remain visible; a tap resumes active interaction.

<a id="Best-practices"></a>
## Best practices
Redact private balances, health details, and sensitive notification content. Retain contextually useful personal information where expected, such as workout metrics or an arrival update; people can disable Always On. Keep primary information legible while dimming secondary detail/fills and simplifying rich images or large color areas. Maintain layout rather than removing controls; use unavailable appearances. Make infrequent subtle updates, such as scores instead of play-by-play, especially on a face-up phone visible peripherally. Ease ongoing animation into rest rather than freezing abruptly.

<a id="Platform-considerations"></a>
## Platform considerations
The source lists iOS/watchOS with no further rules and marks iPadOS/macOS/tvOS/visionOS unsupported.

# Apple HIG: Map user actions to input and alternatives

Use this family to select and compose topic procedures. Infer the platform and
requested work from the supplied project; preserve its framework and constraints.

## Work through the decision

1. Identify the action and available hardware/input context, then enter the keyboard, pointer, focus, eyes, gestures, controller, Pencil, Crown or sensor procedure.
2. Preserve reserved system gestures and familiar input meanings. Separate targeting, focus, selection and activation; define feedback and reachable alternatives for the actions people must complete.
3. Account for comfort, precision, fatigue and switching between available inputs. Custom inputs should not remove an accessible route to an essential action.
4. Deliver an action-to-input mapping, focus/selection states and observable verification scenarios. Keep code/static observations separate from physical comfort, eye/hand and hardware checks.

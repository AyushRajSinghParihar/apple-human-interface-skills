# Apple HIG: status

Choose by meaning: progress indicates an operation completing, a gauge locates a value in a range, a rating expresses rank, and Activity rings exclusively represent personal Move/Exercise/Stand data. Check honesty, accessible labels, cancellation, and the exact platform component before styling.

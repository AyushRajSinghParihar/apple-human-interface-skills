# Scroll view visual examples

Source: [Apple HIG scroll views](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/scroll-views).

## Position and speed are different interactions

The Notes sequence shows content moving through a spatial window while a compact
indicator stays near the middle of its trailing edge. During jog-bar interaction,
tick marks appear around the indicator as the content accelerates or slows.
Dragging this mode adjusts scrolling speed rather than directly setting a
document position. Preserve the system's predictable indicator location and leave
enough content margin for its slightly thicker spatial presentation.

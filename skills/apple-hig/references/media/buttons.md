# Button visual examples

Source: [Apple HIG buttons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/buttons).

## Gaze and dwell feedback

The share-symbol example first highlights its circular button, then reveals the
word Share below it after attention remains on the control. A compact symbol can
therefore retain a discoverable text explanation. Supply a useful label even when
the resting presentation shows only a symbol.

The toolbar example distinguishes available buttons from a dimmed share action.
Attention changes the More button's appearance, and activation opens additional
actions adjacent to it. Preserve separate resting, hover, disabled, and activated
states so looking at a control and invoking its action communicate different
events.

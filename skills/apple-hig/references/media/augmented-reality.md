# Augmented reality visual examples

Source: [Apple HIG augmented reality](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/augmented-reality).

## Object placement

A rotating meteor appears above a real table while the room and the app's content
window remain visible. The person can inspect the object's changing orientation
without losing the physical setting that gives it scale and location. A spatial
object can coexist with ordinary UI and surroundings; presenting one does not
require replacing the entire visible environment.

# Image visual examples

Source: [Apple HIG images](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/images).

## Parallax across layers

The tvOS red-panda artwork tilts while its subject, circular mark, and background
shift in relation to one another. The artwork retains a coherent silhouette
through the different viewpoints. Separate layers should support the intended
sense of depth while leaving adequate room for their movement; a straight-on
preview alone does not reveal cropping or alignment problems during parallax.

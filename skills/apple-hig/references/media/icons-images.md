# Icons: lessons from the visual examples

Source: [Apple HIG — Icons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/icons), independently inspected in the 2026-10-25 snapshot. These are original descriptions; no Apple artwork is included.

## Optical balance

The download glyph has unequal visual mass above and below its center. Equal geometric padding makes it look misplaced in a circular enclosure. Small intentional padding adjustments make the visible glyph feel centered. Evaluate the rendered silhouette rather than relying only on its rectangular bounds.

## Small document artwork

The document example combines a folded-paper mask, a background grid and pulse, a central heart, and a short type label. At smaller sizes, the label and fine grid disappear while the heart and paper silhouette remain. Removing those details is a deliberate size-specific redesign; shrinking the full large illustration would leave noisy, indistinct marks.

The document center-art diagram reserves roughly a tenth of the canvas at each edge. A curved heart can extend slightly into that margin to look balanced. Preserve the intended visual size while allowing small optical corrections.

Source: [Apple HIG materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials).

## Glass responds to the surroundings

The Music window remains readable while its apparent glass brightness changes as
the room becomes darker and the viewpoint changes. Its background continues to
suggest the physical setting without giving every object behind the window equal
visual strength. In visionOS, this adaptive glass treatment responds to surrounding
luminance; the sequence is not a manual light/dark theme switch. Preserve the
system material's adaptation instead of baking in one background appearance.

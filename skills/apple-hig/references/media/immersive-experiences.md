# Immersive experience visual examples

Source: [Apple HIG immersive experiences](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/immersive-experiences).

## Adjusting the surrounding environment

A video window remains visible as the surrounding lakeside environment recedes
and the actual room becomes visible again. The Crown interaction changes the
amount of virtual surroundings without requiring a different primary task.
Support this continuous control of progressive immersion and keep important
content usable across the allowed range, rather than assuming the environment
will always conceal the physical room.

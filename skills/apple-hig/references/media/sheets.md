# Sheet visual examples

Source: [Apple HIG sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets).

## A focused task in front of its parent

The visionOS sequence places a smaller sheet in front of its window and dims the
parent behind it. The parent remains recognizable, but the sheet becomes the
current interaction target. Keep this task attached to its context and centered
comfortably in view rather than sliding it upward from the window's lower edge
or covering the entire parent unnecessarily.

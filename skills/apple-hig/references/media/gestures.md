# Gesture visual examples

Source: [Apple HIG gestures](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/gestures).

## Direct and indirect interaction

For direct interaction, a hand reaches toward a stack of virtual blocks and pushes
its middle block aside. The stack tumbles as the blocks respond near the fingers.
Use direct interaction when the content's physical relationship makes that action
useful and comfortable.

For indirect interaction, the hand remains away from the toolbar while the heart
button highlights and then activates as the person makes a tap gesture. The target
can be selected at a distance without reaching toward the window. Keep this
rested, remote form of interaction available for ordinary interface work.

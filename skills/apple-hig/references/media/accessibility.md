# Accessibility visual examples

Source: [Apple HIG accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility).

## Alternative pointing

The hand-pointer example projects a ray from a resting hand toward the app's
content. Moving the hand redirects the ray across the same window without needing
to touch its surface. The head-pointer example keeps the pointer near the center
of the field of view while the surrounding scene shifts as the person turns.
Both demonstrate ways to aim at existing content through alternatives to eye
targeting. Keep controls operable through the platform's accessible interaction
system rather than making one targeting movement a prerequisite.

# App icon visual examples

Source: [Apple HIG app icons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons).

## Layered depth

As the view moves across the visionOS app launcher, the Photos icon's colored
elements and circular backing reveal their layered relationship. Its recognizable
flower remains legible while the perspective changes. Compose layers as parts of
one clear symbol, and check the result from more than the straight-on viewpoint;
the motion demonstrates depth rather than a reason to add unrelated detail.

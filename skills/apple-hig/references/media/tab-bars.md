# Tab bar visual examples

Source: [Apple HIG tab bars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars).

## Compact destinations with discoverable labels

The visionOS tab bar starts as a narrow column of symbols. Looking at a destination
highlights it; the bar then expands to reveal each destination's title beside its
symbol, and contracts again when attention moves away. Keep useful labels behind
the compact presentation and retain the relationship between a symbol and its
title throughout expansion. A person can target the highlighted destination
before waiting for the explanatory expansion.

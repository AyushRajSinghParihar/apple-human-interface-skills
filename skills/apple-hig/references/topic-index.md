# Apple HIG topic procedures

Use the exact topic procedure for a concrete task. Each procedure links its
supporting article guidance and specifies decisions, work products and checks.
Search the trigger descriptions for the product task, even when the request does
not name an Apple framework. These descriptions help select guidance; they do not
require adopting that framework. Read the owning family and procedure before its
source guide. References supply source detail.

Original [visual explanations](media/index.md) supplement the relevant procedure.
